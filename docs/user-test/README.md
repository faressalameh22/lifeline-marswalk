# Five-minute user test (for the Impact and Validity criteria)

Goal: find out, with real people, whether someone who has never seen LIFELINE can make the planner's decision with it. Record only what actually happens. Do not coach.

## Who

Space Apps rules: do not record or publish names, voices or likenesses of anyone under 18. Quote only adults (18+) who agree in writing that NASA may use their name; otherwise report results without names. Do not collect birthdays, addresses or phone numbers.


3 to 5 people who have not worked on LIFELINE: students, a lecturer, anyone. Ask each one first:

> "We are testing a student project for NASA Space Apps. It takes five minutes. We will note your answers and the time, but not your name unless you say we may use it. Is that OK?"

## Setup

- Open https://faressalameh22.github.io/lifeline-marswalk/ on a laptop or iPad. Reload so the defaults are back.
- Have a stopwatch. Do not explain the tool. Read each task exactly as written.

## Tasks (time each one; stop at 90 s)

| # | Read this out | Correct answer |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Is this Marswalk safe to start? What tells you?" | GO FOR EVA (top right) |
| 2 | "If the rover breaks down right now, how many minutes would the crew have to spare?" | the "Margin if the rover fails here" number, or Rover fails → the minutes shown |
| 3 | "The crew is running late. What should they do at the next stop?" | press Running late → the gate switches to plan B / skips stop B |
| 4 | "Move stop C far away. Is the walk still safe, and why not?" | NO-GO, and the Why NO-GO reason |

## Then ask (write the answer word for word)

1. "On a scale of 1 to 5, how easy was it to tell whether the walk is safe?"
2. "What would you change?"
3. Only if they are a lecturer or engineer: "May we quote one sentence from you, with your name and role?"

## How to report it

Use the real numbers only, for example: "4 of 5 first-time users found the GO/NO-GO verdict in under 10 s; 3 of 5 found the rover-failure margin without help; average ease 4.2/5." Report what went wrong too; it shows the test was real. Quote people only with their permission.
